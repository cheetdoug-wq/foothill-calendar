Questions

How long does it take?

We email you a picture of your calendar within 3 days after your payment comes in. Once you say it looks good, we print and laminate it in 1 to 2 days. Mailed calendars take 1 to 5 business days to arrive.

What if I want something changed?

Reply to the picture we email you with what to change. One round of changes is free. We print only after you say it looks good.

Can I get a refund?

Yes, a full refund any time before we start designing. After that, if something is our mistake (a wrong date, a misspelled name, damage in the mail) we fix it or reprint it for free. Full details are in our terms.

Who can see the moving pictures?

Each calendar gets its own web pages for the animations. They are not listed anywhere or searchable, but anyone who has the link or scans the QR code can open them. If you want names or birthdays left off the online pages, tell us in your order.

How do I pay?

On Venmo to @jake-douglas-44, or cash if you pick up. After you check out, the Pay on Venmo button fills in the amount and your order number. Your order is on hold until the payment comes in, and unpaid orders are canceled after 2 days.

Do you use cookies or track me?

No. No cookies, no ads, no trackers. Read our privacy policy.
