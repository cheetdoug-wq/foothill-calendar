Privacy policy

Effective November 14, 2026

Cheet's Press is a small shop in California that makes custom printed calendars. This policy explains what information we collect on cheetspress.store, what we do with it, and your choices.

What we collect

Only what you type into the order form:

  • Your name, email, and phone number if you give one.
  • Your mailing address, if you choose mail delivery.
  • What goes on the calendar: the name it is for, the months, and any birthdays, dates, names, or descriptions you add. This can include information about other people, so only add what they would be fine with.
  • How you chose to pay (Venmo or cash) and your order number.

We do not collect card numbers or bank details. Venmo payments happen inside Venmo, not on our site.

How we use it

  • To make your calendar, email you updates and a picture to check, and deliver it.
  • To match your Venmo payment to your order.
  • To answer your questions.

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not use it for ads or send you marketing emails.

Who else handles it

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) delivers your order from our site to our email inbox.
  • Google Gmail holds our business email, where orders arrive.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. Like most web hosts, it may keep basic technical logs such as IP addresses.
  • Venmo (PayPal) processes payments you send there, under its own privacy policy.
  • USPS sees your name and address on the envelope if we mail your calendar.

Your calendar's online pages

Each calendar gets its own web pages for the moving pictures that the QR codes open. They are not listed or linked anywhere on our site, but anyone with the link or the QR code can open them. They show what is on your calendar, such as names and special days. If you want names or dates left off the online pages, tell us in your order or by email. You can ask us to take the pages down at any time.

Cookies and tracking

We do not use cookies, analytics, ads, or tracking pixels. While you fill out an order, your answers are kept in your own browser (session storage) so they are still there if you go back to edit. That data stays on your device and clears when you close the tab. Our fonts are stored on our own site, so loading the page does not contact Google. We do not respond to "Do Not Track" signals differently because we do not track you in the first place.

How long we keep it

We keep order emails as long as we need them to run the business and keep records, usually a few years. Ask us and we will delete your information and your calendar's online pages, unless we need to keep a record of the sale.

Your choices

Email cheetspress@gmail.com to see, correct, or delete your information, or to take down your calendar's online pages. We will answer within 30 days.

Kids

Our site is for adults placing orders. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child sent us their information, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
